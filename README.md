# 🌿 ReinforceAI
### AI-Powered Smart Reinforcement Platform for Speech & Language Therapy

> Transforming speech therapy into an engaging, personalized, and rewarding experience using Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 📌 Overview

ReinforceAI is an AI-powered reinforcement system designed to improve motivation and engagement during speech and language therapy sessions.

Unlike traditional reward systems that use generic stars and points, ReinforceAI creates a personalized learning journey where every therapy activity contributes to the child's own interactive reinforcement ecosystem.

The platform helps therapists monitor progress, reward achievements intelligently, and encourage children through gamification and AI-driven reinforcement.

---

# 🚀 Problem Statement

Speech therapists often rely on repetitive and non-personalized reward methods that gradually lose their effectiveness.

Children require:

- Personalized motivation
- Immediate positive reinforcement
- Visual progress tracking
- Fun therapy sessions
- Consistent engagement

ReinforceAI solves this using Artificial Intelligence and Gamification.

## 📷 Magic Scanner

A unique reward system designed for therapists.

### Workflow

1. Therapist draws a symbol on the child's hand
   - ⭐ Star
   - 🌙 Moon
   - 🎵 Music
   - 😊 Smile

2. Child opens the Magic Scanner.

3. AI scans the drawn symbol.

4. Symbol is recognized automatically.

5. Reward points are instantly added.

This makes physical therapy sessions interactive and fun.
